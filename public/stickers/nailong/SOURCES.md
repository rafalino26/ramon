## Walking with a heart — 2026-10-15

`nailong-heart-walk.gif` uses the existing 21-frame animated Nailong sticker from https://soylanet.com/en/pack/A5F3W/ (source: https://stickers.lekker.lat/whatstickers/folders/7465/soylanet-stk-1749372.webp). The character's alternating leg movements are in the source animation; no AI generation or body-part synthesis was used. The original is retained as `nailong-heart-walk-source.webp`.

The transparent union bounds were trimmed, resized to 140×220, and converted to a transparent GIF playing once over 2.8 seconds. Existing marks remain visible. GIF size: 160,670 bytes. `nailong-heart-walk-still.webp` is the last source frame used after arrival and for reduced motion. Details are also recorded in `walk-source.json`. Community distribution is not claimed to be an official release or an unrestricted reuse license.
